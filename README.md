## 🎮 About the Game

Breathedge 2 is a humorous, story-driven space survival adventure developed by RedRuins Softworks and published by HypeTrain Digital. It entered Steam Early Access on August 31, 2026, combining exploration, scavenging, crafting, equipment management, combat, and space survival.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Breathedge 2**. This trainer enhances solo survival by protecting the Man from oxygen depletion, damage, hunger, and resource shortages while exploring wreckage, abandoned stations, settlements, and hazardous regions. It supports the game's core systems, including survival management, crafting, repairs, equipment, inventory use, and combat.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore the story, experiment with crafting and equipment, gather resources freely, or reduce the survival pressure during solo play.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

- **Infinite Oxygen** — Keeps the oxygen supply from decreasing during exploration and scavenging.
- **Infinite Health** — Prevents health loss from environmental hazards and hostile encounters.

- **Unlimited Inventory Items** — Provides unlimited quantities for selected inventory resources and consumable items.
- **Free Crafting** — Allows crafting tools, equipment, survival supplies, and upgrades without consuming required materials.
- **Unlimited Equipment Durability** — Prevents equipped tools and protective equipment from losing durability.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **One-Hit Defeat** — Allows crafted weapons and combat tools to defeat hostile creatures and robots with a single successful attack.
- **Damage Protection** — Protects the player while fighting enemies across wreckage, stations, and planetary areas.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Load a save and enter active gameplay before enabling trainer options. Infinite oxygen is useful for extended movement through open space, wreckage, abandoned stations, and other hazardous areas. Keep normal movement controls unchanged and disable the trainer before returning to the main menu.

### Tools & Inventory

Use unlimited inventory items selectively when gathering scrap, repair materials, oxygen supplies, food, and crafting components. Open the inventory only after the game has finished loading the current area. Free crafting is intended for testing recipes, tools, equipment, and upgrades without consuming stored materials.

### World & Environment

Breathedge 2 focuses on exploration across dangerous space environments and other regions connected to the story. Infinite oxygen and protection from environmental damage make it easier to investigate points of interest, search wreckage, complete objectives, and collect useful or unusual items without repeatedly returning to a safe area.

### Combat & Weapons

Hostile creatures and robots can be encountered during exploration. Enable Damage Protection before entering a fight, then use One-Hit Defeat with the game's available weapons and combat tools. Combat remains optional in some situations, so disable offensive options when you want to preserve the normal survival experience.
